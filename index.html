Common Questions

Got questions?
Here are the answers.

Do I need a big TikTok following to make money on TikTok Shop? ↓
Absolutely not. TikTok Shop pushes your shoppable videos directly to people who are already browsing that product — regardless of your follower count. Monica has made sales on videos with 1 like. You don't need fame. You need the right product, the right tag, and consistency.
I'm not tech-savvy. Will I be able to keep up? ↓
Yes. Everything inside the Academy is taught step-by-step, screen by screen. If you can send a text message and scroll TikTok, you can do this. Monica also teaches you AI tools that make the technical stuff easier — not harder.
Can I do this while still working my 9-to-5? ↓
That's exactly what this community is built for. Monica built her entire TikTok Shop and UGC business while still employed — before work, after work, and during her 5-to-9. The content batching system inside the Academy lets you film a full week of content in 2 hours on Sunday.
What if I don't know what niche I'm in? ↓
That's one of the first things we tackle inside the Academy. Your niche is hiding in your everyday life — what you buy, what you do for work, what people ask you for help with. The free Mission Kit skills audit on this site is a great place to start. Take it before you join.
Can I cancel anytime? ↓
Yes — cancel anytime inside your Skool settings. No contracts, no commitments, no questions asked. But fair warning — once you see your first TikTok Shop commission notification, you won't want to leave.
